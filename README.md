# UpRush

**A vertical mobile platformer built with Godot 4.**

Climb as high as you can, jumping from platform to platform as you race upward. Collect power-ups and runes along the way, and blast enemies with spell bolts to clear your path. One wrong step and you fall, so keep climbing and beat your best height.

UpRush is the follow-up to my earlier game, Sky Rush, adding combat, spells and a deeper power-up system.
